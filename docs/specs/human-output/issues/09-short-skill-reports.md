**What to build:** each reporting skill opens with three to six lines in the person's language (spec, *Short first*, *The skills' short report*). `/binnacle:product`, `/binnacle:daily`, `/binnacle:status`, `/binnacle:task` without `run`, and `/binnacle:audit` follow the spec's order, skill by skill; leave out a section with nothing in it; give the next command alone in a fenced block; end with one line on what else they can show; and give the rest when the person asks. Their instructions stay English and list the spec's Russian terms for the model. No attention code in a person's text: an item is worded from its code, message and `params`. A calculated value takes its one-word mark, an unknown "no data". `docs/03-mcp-and-skills.md` describes the short report.
